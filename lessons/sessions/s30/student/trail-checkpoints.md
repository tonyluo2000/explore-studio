## What to observe

- Nova arrives in Moon Meadow. Walk to the object or character’s readable prompt;
  press and release E once for each action. All positions, colors and words still
  come from your validated package.
- Lamps visibly switch OFF/ON with their authored colors. Each lamp keeps its own
  Boolean state, including when multiple lamps share one style.
- Devices illuminate one cell per charging action up to their authored goal;
  numeric counts can continue afterward. Different devices keep separate counts.
- Ordinary instruments have distinct engraved silhouettes and a white visit tick.
  A sequence keeper’s three small cells follow the real prefix and reset state.
- Read the complete authored character line and its focused echo. Talking does not
  secretly toggle, charge, move objects or rewrite the rule.
